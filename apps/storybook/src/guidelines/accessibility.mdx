# Accessibility

Every story in this Storybook runs `@storybook/addon-a11y` in **`test: "error"`** mode.

## Standards

- **WCAG 2.1 AA contrast:** `>= 4.5:1` for normal text and `>= 3.0:1` for UI borders, icons, and chart marks — enforced automatically at token build/save time by `@manovaspace/tokens`.
- **Target sizes:** Touch controls use `size="touch"` / `size="icon-touch"` (`44 × 44 px`); compact desktop interactive controls remain at least `24 × 24 px`.
- **Keyboard & focus:** All interactive controls are reachable via `Tab`, expose visible `:focus-visible` rings (`--ring`), keep `scroll-margin-block-start` clear of sticky `ShellHeader` bars, and trap focus inside `Dialog`, `AlertDialog`, `Sheet`, and `ProgressiveDialog`.
- **Motion & forced colors:** Components respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (`useReducedMotion()` / `--motion: 0ms`) and maintain visible borders (`CanvasText`) and focus outlines (`Highlight`) under `forced-colors: active`.

## Selective primitive adoption (no big-bang rewrite)

`@manovaspace/ui` keeps `radix-ui` primitives and `react-date-object` / `react-multi-date-picker` for existing controls and Jalali/Gregorian calendars (validated by full unit, keyboard, and Storybook `play` tests). Base UI and `@internationalized/date` are adopted **selectively** only for net-new complex primitives that Radix lacks, never via a blanket rewrite.
