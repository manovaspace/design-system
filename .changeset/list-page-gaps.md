`ListPage` (experimental) closes its gaps so a product can render list pages with props only. New: a clear-all control in the chip row (shown with two or more chips); a `framed` variant with bordered card controls; a numeric active-filter badge with `activeCount` and `formatCount`; arrow-key, Home and End focus movement in the sort sheet, with the selected option focused on open; chip enter and leave motion that respects reduced motion; `width` (`default`, `wide`, `full`) and `inset` (`page`, `shell`) options, with the `--list-page-gutter`, `--list-page-fab-clearance` and `--list-page-bottom-padding` CSS hooks; an `onOpenChange` callback on the filter sheet; and sticky toolbar spacing of 16 px above the search, 12 px below the controls, then a 1 px divider.
