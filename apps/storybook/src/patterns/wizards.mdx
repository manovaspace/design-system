# Wizards & sheets

Multi-step flows and record editors standardize on `ProgressiveDialog`, `FormSheet`, `EditFieldSheet`, `FilterSheet`, and `ConfirmDialog`.

## Sheet & dialog rules

- **Responsive presentation:** `FormSheet` and `ProgressiveDialog` render full-screen sheets below `md` and centered dialogs from `md`.
- **Title parity:** Sheet or dialog title equals the trigger label that opened it.
- **Footer button order:** Secondary/cancel action first (`variant="outline"` or `variant="ghost"`), primary action last. Keep the footer above the virtual keyboard and inside safe area insets (`useKeyboardInset`).
- **Multi-step wizards:** Show identical step counts and progress indicators (`steps`, `stepIndex`) across mobile and desktop, with one primary action per step and inline `error` + `errorAction` retry when a step fails.
- **Destructive confirmation:** Use `ConfirmDialog` (`AlertDialog` wrapper) or `HoldToConfirmButton` for irreversible deletions.
